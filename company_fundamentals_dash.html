Company fundamentals dashboard

Enter a company's figures and market data. The app calculates profitability, financial position, returns and valuation in code, so every number is exact, and checks the data. Claude then explains the results using a written Skill (SKILL.md). It describes the numbers and never gives buy, sell or hold advice.
